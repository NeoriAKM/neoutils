lsdr

1.4 | 20.08.2026

One of the first util of every time! ls in UNIX, dir in POSIX. This ls realisation differs with short and simple code. And LSDR have colorful output. you can turn it off, if you want to use classic white output, with flag -no-color.

Requirements

  • C language compiler (GCC or clang)
  • glibc (if dynamic, else skip this)
